About FoodLens

FoodLens is an AI-powered food inspection platform designed to help users understand the visible condition of fruits and vegetables through image-based analysis.

Our Purpose

Judging food by eye is something everyone does, but it is easy to miss early changes. FoodLens gives you a second look: a quick, consistent check of how a fruit or vegetable appears right now, so you can decide what to do next.

How It Works

Three steps from photo to result.

1. Capture or Upload

Take a photo with your camera or upload an image of a fruit or vegetable.

2. AI Analysis

The image is analyzed for the food type and its visible condition.

3. Inspection Insight

See the condition, a confidence percentage and when to inspect again.

AI-Based Inspection

FoodLens looks at visual cues such as colour, texture and surface changes, then estimates whether the food looks Fresh or Rotten and how confident it is about that estimate.

  • Works from a single photo
  • Shows a confidence percentage with every result
  • Saves each inspection to your history

Why Food Quality Matters

Small checks make everyday food choices easier.

Less waste

Spotting changes early helps you use food while it is still good.

Better choices

A clear result helps you decide whether to eat, store or discard.

Consistent checks

The same kind of check every time, recorded in your history.

FoodLens gives a visual estimate from a photo. It cannot detect things you cannot see, so use your own judgement and normal food-safety guidance.
